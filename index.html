DNA Sequencing Technologies: Mechanisms and Workflows

Interactive figures of first-, second- and third-generation sequencing, from library preparation to data output.

Each page is a hand-drawn, three-dimensional figure that can be rotated, operated step by step and viewed in cross-section. The figures follow a sample from extracted DNA to the sequence files delivered to the compute cluster (Farm, UC Davis). Begin with library preparation and select a platform; every page links to the next step and back to this overview.

Second generation: short-read sequencing by cyclic imaging reviewed

2a. Illumina flow cellHybridisation to the P5/P7 lawn, bridge amplification into clusters, sequencing by synthesis, index reads and the paired-end turnaround.
3a. Illumina sequencerLoading the flow cell and reagents, a 2 x 150-cycle run, and a cross-section of the fluidics and optics. Linked to the AVITI instrument for comparison.
2b. AVITI flow cellCircular library on a surface primer, rolling circle amplification into a polony, sequencing by avidity, and the path from signal to base call.

Illumina: bridge amplification; the labelled nucleotide that is imaged is also incorporated
AVITI: rolling circle amplification; an avidite is imaged, then an unlabelled nucleotide is incorporated

Third generation: single-molecule long-read sequencing in preparation: draft, under review

These pages are drafts under review and are not yet verified in full; the nanopore flow cell page in particular requires further detail.

1a. SMRTbell library preparationShearing to 15-20 kb, repair and A-tailing, hairpin adapters on both ends, nuclease treatment, size selection, primer annealing and polymerase binding.
2a. PacBio SMRT Cell and zero-mode waveguideA single polymerase at the base of a 70 nm well; phospholinked nucleotides fluoresce as they are incorporated; repeated passes around the circular template yield a HiFi consensus read.
3a. Revio-style sequencerFour independent stages for four SMRT Cells, with on-board GPUs generating HiFi reads and methylation calls. 1b. Nanopore library preparationOptional fragmentation or size selection, end preparation and nick repair, then either direct adapter ligation (single sample) or native barcoding followed by adapter ligation (multiplexed), clean-up, priming and loading.
2b. Nanopore flow cellPriming and loading step by step (flow cell check, priming port, draw-back, 800 + 200 uL priming mix, 75 uL library dropwise onto the SpotON port, light shield), then a single well: the motor protein feeds one strand through the pore and the current trace is basecalled.
3b. MinION and PromethIONA MinION Mk1D connected to a laptop and a PromethION 24 with on-board GPUs: the same pore and chemistry at one and at twenty-four flow cells.
PacBio HiFi: no amplification; real-time single-molecule reading; consensus of repeated passes
Nanopore: a motor protein feeds a single strand through a protein pore; the ionic current trace is basecalled
